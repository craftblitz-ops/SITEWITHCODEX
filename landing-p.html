Yes, genuinely free — we review your last 6 months of accounts and tell you where you stand. There's no obligation to become a client afterward. We offer this because most people who see the review choose to continue with us, and we'd rather earn that than ask you to commit blind.

How much does ongoing filing cost after the free review?

It depends on your income sources and business complexity. You'll get a clear quote after the free review — in writing, before any billable work starts.

What documents do I need to get started?

Typically your PAN, Aadhaar, last year's ITR (if any), bank statements, and Form 26AS/AIS. For GST, your registration certificate and sales/purchase records. We'll confirm the exact list once we understand your situation.
